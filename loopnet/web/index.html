01 — THE CLASSIC

A simple connection.

Two players take turns dropping a disc into one of seven columns. Each disc falls to the lowest empty space.

Connect four of your discs horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win. If the board fills up first, it’s a draw.

Play with keys 1–7. U undoes a move. No timer—take your time.
